# Cutrium — Milestone 7 Decision Content

## Hard Entry Gate
Do not begin unless the repository explicitly records a positive human review after Milestones 4–6. The record must approve the reward loop, theme pipeline, Hunter/Pulse, Freeze/Instant Barrier, and full decision-content production. If absent, pending, TUNE, or STOP: stop without changes.

## Purpose
Produce approximately 10–12 standard levels plus one special final level using only approved systems.

## Principles
One persistent scene; data-driven definitions; typical human time near 20–45 seconds; early mistakes tolerated; teach before combining; vary spawn/count/type/speed/barrier/target/powers rather than speed alone; no boss framework or final-only rule.

## Teaching Arc
Inspect current content and build a clear arc covering basic capture, vulnerable barrier, grouping, reward encouragement, Hunter, Pulse, Freeze, Instant Barrier, mixed levels, mastery, and a finale composed only from approved systems. Exact count/order must be reported and duplication avoided.

## Development Navigation
Development-only level jump/restart/previous/next and validation summary. No production map/select screen.

## Evidence
For every level record intended decision, configuration, target, expected time, observed human time when available, cheese strategy, and failure tolerance. Automated simulation cannot prove fun; separate structural checks from human evidence.

## Validation
Unique IDs, legal board/targets/spawns, references, supported speeds, power/theme/feedback references, deterministic conversion/reset, seeded invariant smoke runs, and Play Mode initialization/Retry/forced-completion/Next/presenter reconciliation for every level. Run complete suites.

## Manual Gate
Require human full-sequence phone/tablet-profile playtest and record time, attempts, breaks, confusion, fatigue, repetition, and bugs. Codex must not claim it.

## Acceptance
Approved count; no scene duplication; mechanics taught before combination; early failure not harsh; final uses existing systems; tests/compiler/protected files clean; human testing honestly marked pending if absent.
